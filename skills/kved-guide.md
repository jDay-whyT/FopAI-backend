# KVED Guide for FOP
# Source: KVED DK 009:2010, PKU Art. 291, DPS clarifications

## Critical Rules

- FOP can ONLY earn income from KVEDs listed in their registration (ZIR 107.01.01)
- Income from non-registered KVED = violation → back taxes + penalties
- Can register multiple KVEDs — one is "primary" (основний)
- Group 1-2: restricted KVED list (see below)
- Group 3: all KVEDs except prohibited list (PKU Art. 291.5)
- KVED must match payment purpose in bank — mismatch = finmon trigger

## Groups 1-2: Allowed Activities

### Group 1 ONLY (PKU Art. 291.4.1):
- Retail trade at markets (physical markets only)
- Basic household services to population:
  - Shoe repair, clothing repair
  - Hairdressing
  - Photography
  - Furniture repair
  - Watch/clock repair
  - Bicycle repair
  - Other basic services

### Group 2 Allowed (PKU Art. 291.4.2):
- All services to: general public OR other EP taxpayers
- Production and sale of goods
- Restaurant/food service business
- **Cannot serve:** general tax system legal entities (with some exceptions)

### Group 2 Specific Restrictions:
❌ Cannot be Group 2 (must use Group 3):
- Internet service providers
- Real estate mediation (KVED 70.31)
- Jewelry production/sale (precious metals/stones)

---

## Prohibited for ALL EP Groups (PKU Art. 291.5)

❌ These KVEDs are FORBIDDEN on simplified system:
- **K section** — Financial intermediation, currency exchange, insurance
- Gambling, lotteries, betting
- Mineral/oil/gas extraction
- Import of excisable goods (alcohol, tobacco, fuel — import specifically)
- Post office activities (except courier)
- Licensed notary services
- Licensed advocacy services
- Licensed audit services (as primary)
- Arms/weapons trade

## KVED + Bank Compliance

**Payment purpose must match KVED:**

| KVED | Safe payment description |
|------|-------------------------|
| 49.32 | "Payment for taxi services per agreement" |
| 62.01 | "Payment for software development services per contract" |
| 73.11 | "Payment for advertising services per contract №..." |
| 63.12 | "Platform content monetization payout" |
| 74.90 | "Payment for professional services per agreement" |

**Mismatch examples that trigger bank request:**
- KVED 62.01 (IT) but receiving payment "for goods" → mismatch
- KVED 73.11 (advertising) but receiving "personal transfer" → mismatch
- KVED 49.32 (taxi) but receiving from multiple individuals without platform → suspicious

## How to Register/Change KVED

**Adding KVED:**
- Submit Form 6 to state registrar (free, online via Diia)
- Effective from date of submission
- No limit on number of KVEDs

**Removing KVED:**
- Same Form 6
- Check: if income received from this KVED in current quarter — keep until quarter end

**Changing primary KVED:**
- Affects reporting category
- May affect EP group eligibility

## Bot Response Rules for KVED Questions

1. Always check FOP group first — Group 1-2 have restrictions
2. For bloggers: always recommend 73.11 + 63.12 (not 90.03 alone)
3. For taxi: 49.32 is mandatory, not optional
4. For IT: 62.01 or 62.02 depending on actual work type
5. For general freelance: 74.90 is safest catch-all
6. If KVED doesn't match activity → recommend adding correct KVED immediately
7. Payment purpose in bank must contain KVED-relevant description
