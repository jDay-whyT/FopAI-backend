# PKU Rules for FOP — Tax Code of Ukraine
# Source: PKU 2755-VI, DPS clarifications, official forms (2026 actual)

## Core Principle

FOP (sole proprietor) can operate under:
1. **Simplified system (EP)** — Groups 1, 2, 3 (see ep-groups.md for rates/limits)
2. **General system** — 18% PDFO + 5% VZ + ESV on net profit

---

## Income Recognition Rules

### Simplified System (EP) — PKU Art. 292

**Income = everything received on FOP account:**
- Bank receipts (cash + non-cash)
- In-kind received goods/services at market value
- Freely received assets (gifts) at market value
- Expired accounts payable (Group 3 VAT-payers only)

**NOT income (PKU Art. 292.11):**
- VAT amounts (VAT-registered FOP)
- Return of bank deposits previously taxed
- Loans received (until default)
- Agency payments (money received on behalf of third party)
- Returned prepayments/goods
- Budget grants (taxed separately as individual)
- Exchange rate difference on foreign currency (PKU Art. 292.5)

**Date of income:** date of actual receipt on account (cash method).

### General System — PKU Art. 177

**Income = gross revenue** (cash + non-cash + natural form).
**Tax base = net income** = revenue minus documented business expenses.
**Date:** actual receipt date (cash method).

**Documented expenses must be:**
- Business-related
- Confirmed by primary documents
- Actually paid (cash method)

---

## Expense Documentation (General System & V2+)

**Valid primary documents:**
- Invoice (rakhunok-faktura)
- Act of completion (akt pryimannia-peredachi)
- Cash register receipt (RRO/PRRO receipt)
- Bank payment confirmation
- Bill of lading (nakладна) for goods

**Storage period:** 3 years after end of reporting period (PKU Art. 44.1). Recommended: keep until audit completed.

**Fixed assets (основні засоби):**
- Cost over 20,000 UAH = fixed asset, depreciated
- Below 20,000 UAH = expensed immediately
- Depreciation methods: straight-line, accelerated

## Reporting Obligations

### EP Groups 1-2 (Annual Declaration)
- Form: Tax declaration of EP taxpayer (updated 31.01.2025, Order 578)
- Period: annual
- Deadline: 60 days after year end (by March 1)
- Content: income by quarter, advance EP payments, KVED codes, employee count

### EP Group 3 (Quarterly Declaration)
- Form: same tax declaration
- Period: quarterly
- Deadline: 40 days after quarter end
- Content: income for quarter, EP amount (5% or 3%)

### Combined Quarterly Report (from 2026) — ALL FOP with employees
- PDFO + VZ + ESV with monthly breakdown
- Deadline: 40 days after quarter end
- Q1 2026 → 11.05.2026
- Q2 2026 → 10.08.2026

### General System (Annual + Quarterly Advances)
- Annual property and income declaration
- Deadline: May 1 of following year
- Tax payment: August 1 of following year
- Quarterly advances: by 20th of month after quarter

## VAT (PDV) Rules

**Mandatory VAT registration:**
- Revenue exceeds 1,000,000 UAH in last 12 months
- Applies to: general system FOP and Group 3 voluntarily

**VAT rate:** 20% (standard), 7% (medical, some food), 0% (export)

**Group 3 with VAT:** EP rate = 3% (instead of 5%)
**Group 3 without VAT:** EP rate = 5%

**VAT NOT applicable:** Groups 1 and 2 (cannot register for VAT)

---

## KVED (Activity Classification) Rules

**Critical rules:**
- FOP can only receive income from activities listed in their registration data
- Income from non-registered activity = violation (DPS clarification ZIR 107.01.01)
- Can register multiple KVEDs — one is "primary"
- Group 2: can only serve public and other EP taxpayers (with exceptions)

**KVED selection by activity:**
- Taxi/transport: 49.32 (taxi services), 49.39 (other passenger transport)
- IT/software: 62.01 (software development), 62.02 (IT consulting)
- Blogger/content: 73.11 (advertising agencies), 63.12 (web portals), 59.11 (video production)
- Freelancer/services: 74.90 (other professional services)
- Trade: 47.xx (retail), 46.xx (wholesale)
- Restaurant: 56.xx (food and beverage)

**Group 3 allowed KVEDs:** all except prohibited list (PKU Art. 291.5)

## Common Violations & Penalties

| Violation | Consequence | Penalty |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Exceeded EP limit | Move to general system | 15% EP on excess |
| Wrong KVED (prohibited activity) | EP cancelled from quarter | Back taxes |
| No RRO/PRRO when required | Fine | 100% of operation amount (first time), 150% (repeat) |
| Late EP payment | Penalty | 20% of amount + daily interest |
| Late ESV payment | Penalty | 20% of amount + daily interest |
| Late declaration | Fine | 340 UAH (first), 1,020 UAH (repeat) |
| No primary documents | Income considered understated | Full tax + 25% penalty |
| Paying salary below minimum | Fine | 10 minimum wages per employee |

## Electronic Cabinet of Taxpayer

**Key functions (DPS electronic cabinet):**
- Submit declarations online
- Check tax debt/overpayment
- View EP/ESV payment history
- Request individual tax consultation (IPK)
- Check EP registration status

**Access:** id.gov.ua (Diia) or EDS (electronic digital signature)

---

## Individual Tax Consultations (IPK)

**When to recommend IPK:**
- Non-standard situation not covered by general rules
- Conflicting DPS clarifications
- New type of activity without clear regulation
- Crypto operations
- Foreign income structure

**How to get IPK:**
- Submit request via electronic cabinet
- Response within 25 business days
- IPK protects taxpayer if followed correctly (PKU Art. 53)
