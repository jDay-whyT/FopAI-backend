# EP Groups — Limits, Rates & Rules (2026)
# Source: PKU Art. 291-295, DPS official data, Budget Law 2026

## Base Social Standards (2026)
- Minimum wage (MZP): **8,647 UAH/month** (from 01.01.2026)
- Subsistence minimum (PM) for working persons: **3,328 UAH/month**
- ESV minimum: **1,902.34 UAH/month** (22% of MZP, mandatory for all FOP)

---

## Group 1

**Who qualifies (PKU Art. 291.4.1):**
- No hired workers
- Only: retail trade at markets OR basic household services to public
- Cannot serve legal entities or other FOP

**Annual income limit:** 1,444,049 UAH (167 × MZP 8,647)

**Monthly payments:**
| Tax | Amount | Note |
|-----|--------|------|
| Single tax (EP) | max 332.80 UAH | 10% of PM — set by local council |
| Military levy (VZ) | 864.70 UAH | 10% of MZP |
| ESV | 1,902.34 UAH | 22% of MZP |
| **TOTAL/month** | **~3,099 UAH** | |

**Key rules:**
- EP and VZ fixed for entire year based on Jan 1 MZP — do not recalculate mid-year
- Local council may set lower EP rate — always check FOP's region
- Annual total: ~37,198 UAH

---

## Group 2

**Who qualifies (PKU Art. 291.4.2):**
- Up to 10 hired workers
- Services to: general public OR other EP taxpayers
- Production/sale of goods, restaurant business
- Cannot serve general tax system legal entities (with exceptions)

**Annual income limit:** 7,211,598 UAH (834 × MZP 8,647)

**Monthly payments:**
| Tax | Amount | Note |
|-----|--------|------|
| Single tax (EP) | max 1,729.40 UAH | 20% of MZP — set by local council |
| Military levy (VZ) | 864.70 UAH | 10% of MZP |
| ESV | 1,902.34 UAH | 22% of MZP |
| **TOTAL/month** | **~4,496 UAH** | |

**Key rules:**
- EP and VZ fixed for entire year
- ESV changes if MZP changes during year
- Annual total: ~53,957 UAH
- Prohibited: internet providers, real estate mediation, jewelry

---

## Group 3

**Who qualifies (PKU Art. 291.4.3):**
- Any number of hired workers
- Any activity type (except prohibited list PKU Art. 291.5)
- Any counterparties: individuals, FOP, legal entities, non-residents
- Best for: IT, freelancers, bloggers, platform workers, foreign income

**Annual income limit:** 10,091,049 UAH (1,167 × MZP 8,647)

**Quarterly payments (% of actual income):**
| Tax | Rate | Note |
|-----|------|------|
| Single tax (EP) | **5%** of income | Without VAT registration |
| Single tax (EP) | **3%** of income | With VAT registration |
| Military levy (VZ) | **1%** of income | Quarterly |
| ESV | 1,902.34 UAH/month | Fixed regardless of income |

**Example (income 45,000 UAH/month):**
- EP: 45,000 × 5% = 2,250 UAH
- VZ: 45,000 × 1% = 450 UAH
- ESV: 1,902.34 UAH
- Total: ~4,602 UAH/month (~10.2% effective rate)

## General Tax System

**Taxes (PKU Art. 177):**
| Tax | Rate | Base |
|-----|------|------|
| PDFO | 18% | Net income (revenue minus documented expenses) |
| Military levy (VZ) | 5% | Net income |
| ESV | 22% | Net income, min 1,902.34 UAH/month if income exists |

**Key rule:** Tax base = revenue minus documented business expenses. No expenses = tax on full revenue.

---

## What Counts as Income (PKU Art. 292)

**IS income for EP taxpayer:**
- Cash received (bank account or cash)
- Non-cash/in-kind received goods/services
- Freely received goods (gifts, donations) — at market value
- Expired accounts payable (Group 3 with VAT only)
- Bonuses received on FOP account

**NOT income for EP taxpayer (PKU Art. 292.11):**
- VAT amounts (for VAT-registered FOP)
- Return of previously taxed deposits from bank
- Budget grants (taxed separately as individual income at 18%+VZ)
- Loans received (until repayment deadline missed)
- Return of goods sold / prepayments returned
- Funds received as agent (on behalf of third party)

**Foreign currency income:**
- Taxed at NBU exchange rate on date of receipt
- Exchange rate difference NOT included in income (PKU Art. 292.5)

## Limit Exceeded — Consequences

| Situation | Consequence | Payment |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Group 1 limit exceeded | Move to Group 2 or 3 | 15% EP on excess amount |
| Group 2 limit exceeded | Move to Group 3 | 15% EP on excess amount |
| Group 3 limit exceeded | Move to General System | 15% EP on excess amount |
| Prohibited KVED activity | EP cancelled from quarter start | Back taxes |
| Wrong activity type | EP cancelled | Back taxes |

**Bot alert thresholds:**
- 70% of annual limit → warning notification
- 90% of annual limit → urgent notification + recommended actions
- 100% reached → critical + next steps

## Payment Deadlines

| Payment | Groups 1-2 | Group 3 | General System |
|---------|-----------|---------|----------------|
| EP | By 20th current month | 50 days after quarter | Quarterly advance |
| VZ | By 20th current month | 40 days after quarter | 40 days after quarter |
| ESV | By 20th of month after quarter | By 20th of month after quarter | 40 days after year |

**Reporting (from 2026):** Quarterly combined report (PDFO + VZ + ESV with monthly breakdown)
- Q1 2026 → deadline 11.05.2026
- Q2 2026 → deadline 10.08.2026
- Q3 2026 → deadline 09.11.2026
- Q4 2026 → deadline 09.02.2027

## FOP Closing Checklist (from official document)

1. Check tax debt and ESV arrears
2. Dismiss hired workers, make final payments
3. Submit Form 12 to state registrar
4. Submit final declarations (EP or income declaration, ESV report Form D5)
5. Pay all outstanding taxes and ESV
6. Cancel VAT registration if applicable
7. Close bank accounts, get 3-year transaction statements
8. Cancel RRO/PRRO registration if applicable
9. Cancel alcohol/tobacco license if applicable
10. Keep all documents and accounting books for 3 years
11. Monitor EDR database for "terminated" status

## Prohibited Activities for ALL EP Groups (PKU Art. 291.5)

- Currency exchange
- Financial intermediation, insurance
- Gambling, lotteries
- Mineral/oil/gas extraction
- Import of excisable goods
- Notary, advocacy (as main licensed activity)
- Audit services (as main activity)

---

## FOP with Employee — Additional

From each employee salary FOP must:
- Withhold: PDFO 18% + VZ 5% (from salary)
- Pay additionally: ESV 22% (at FOP expense, on salary amount)
- Minimum salary: not less than MZP 8,647 UAH

Deadlines: PDFO+VZ on salary payment day; ESV by 20th next month.

---

## Bot Notes

1. EP and VZ Groups 1-2: fixed Jan 1 — never recalculate mid-year
2. ESV = 22% CURRENT MZP — increases if MZP changes
3. Payment IBAN depends on FOP's registered tax address — update if FOP moves to another district
4. FOP pays ESV even with zero income (exceptions: military service, maternity leave with documents)
5. Group 3: tax calculated quarterly on actual income received — zero income quarter = zero EP/VZ
6. Grant income: not EP income, taxed as individual at 18% PDFO + VZ
